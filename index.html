shorts-kit

shorts-kit is the private publishing tool of one independent creator. It runs on his own computers and publishes the short educational videos he makes to his own YouTube channels: Innerframe (3D animations of how real machines work), What If Exactly (animated science scenarios built on sourced figures) and Open Source Radar.

It uses the YouTube API Services to upload those videos, to post one comment under each of them from the channel, and to read the channels' own statistics. It does not access any other account and is not offered to anyone else.

By using shorts-kit you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the privacy policy, the terms and the Google Privacy Policy.
