Privacy policy

shorts-kit uses YouTube API Services. Its only user is the owner of the YouTube channels Innerframe, What If Exactly and Open Source Radar, who runs it on his own computers. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

With the owner's consent, given through Google's OAuth screen, shorts-kit uploads the owner's videos to his channels with their titles, descriptions and tags, posts one comment from the channel under each of them, and reads the channels' own data: their videos and their YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, retention). It never reads or stores data about other users or other channels.

The OAuth tokens are kept only on the owner's computers, in files readable by his account alone. The statistics of his own videos are kept in local files, for his own use, so he can compare his videos. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising. shorts-kit stores no cookies and no data on anyone's device. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page, security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking it stops shorts-kit at once; its local copies are then deleted.
