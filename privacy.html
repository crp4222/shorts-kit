Privacy policy

shorts-kit is used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Open Source Radar and Innerframe, on the owner's own computers.

With the owner's consent it reads those channels' YouTube data (videos, comments, YouTube Analytics reports) and posts comments under their own videos. It does not access anyone else's account.

The access tokens are stored only on the owner's computers. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. shorts-kit's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Last updated: 10 November 2026.
